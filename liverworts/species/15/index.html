HERBERTUS_BIOGEOGRAPHY_2006 (1 entry)

distribution: Distribution: New Zealand and Tasmania. Placed in Clade A of the genus in phylogenetic analysis. Sister to H. juniperoideus (Neotropical/African clade). ITS sequence divergence from H. juniperoideus 5.0–9.3%; split estimated at 9–17 Ma. Two New Zealand accessions used (Glenny 9584, Engel 18886). Current trans-oceanic distribution between New Zealand and Tasmania cannot be explained by Gondwanan vicariance alone — a combination of short-distance dispersal, rare long-distance dispersal, and extinction/recolonization is invoked. [Source: Feldberg et al. 2006, Phylogenetic biogeography of the leafy liverwort Herbertus. J Biogeography 33: 2168–2182]

Comments: This species differs from Triandrophyllum subtrifidum in a number of respects, including aspect, leaf orientation and symmetry.
