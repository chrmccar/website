              VANDIEMENIACEAE: A NEW FAMILY IN
                      THE METZGERIALES

                                    HELEN J. HEWSON 1


      A new monotypic family is described from Tasmania. It is close to the Aneur-
 aceae and Metzgeriaceae.
      Vandiemeniaceae Hewson/am. nov. Typus: Vandiemenia ratkowskiana.
      Plantae thallinae. Thallus ecostatus, Riccardia-formis. Rami masculi ventrales,
 Metzgeria-formes. Plantae feminae ignotae. Sporophyta ignota.
      Vandiemenia ratkowskiana Hewson gen. & sp. nov.
      Plantae thallinae pulvinatae. Thallus ecostatus, c. 1 cm x 0.1-2.0 mm, irregulariter
 bipinnatim furcatus, marginibus acutis, cuticula laevi. Rami masculi ventrales ut in
Metzgeria, plerumque unistratosi, cum antheridia superficiales in medio. Typus : V.
ratkowskiana Hewson. Holotypus : Milles Track ad Wellington Falls, Mt. Wellington,
Hobart, A. V. Ratkowsky 80/119, 1 I Jan. 1980 (CANB).
      Plants in cushions on rotten log. Thalli c. 1 cm long, 0.2-1.0 mm wide, nerveless,
 branched; branching irregular, bipinnate, of two types - apical and pseudo-dichotomous
and ventral, apparently a modified male branch; margin acute, winged, somewhat crenulate-
sinuate; wing 1- 3 cells wide; transverse section piano- to biconvex, 4--7 cells thick; apex
not dissected, protected by ventral non-persistent mucilage papillae; cuticle smooth; rhizoids
ventral. (Oil bodies not known, 'not obvious' according to D . A. Ratkowsky.) Gemmae
unknown . Dioecious : female plants unknown. Male plants with numerous ventral branch-
es: branches deeply cucullate to pseudo-spherical, mostly one cell thick with laciniate margin
tending to have epidermal cells produced into hairs; midrib region bearing antherida on
dorsal surface; antherida not sunken. Sporophyte unknown .
      The family and genus are named in honour of Van Diemen after whom Tasmania
was originally named. The species is named in honour of Ann Ratkowsky who
collected the plant.
      This species has a gametophyte with the vegetative morphology of members of
the genus Riccardia in the Aneuraceae. However, its male branches are similar to
those of Metzgeria in the Metzgeriaceae. Even in the absence of female and sporo-
phyte material it is clear that a new family is at hand. One is lead to speculate that
the Yandiemeniaceae represent a relict of a possible ancestor linki ng the modern
Aneuraceae and Metzgeriaceae. Certainly this family presents consolidating evidence
for the taxon Metzgeriineae in the sense of Schuster (1979), who ranks it as a suborder.
      Schuster (1966) presented some evolutionary speculations in discussion about
Phy llothallia. He draws attention to the Southern Hemisphere proving to be a
probable centre for the evolution of a polytypic Metzgerialean flora. Indeed Van-

                                                              SUMMARY

             The endemic Tasmanian hepatic Vandiemenia ratkowskiana Hewson, originally referred to the family
             Vandiemeniaceae (Marchantiophyta), is restudied taxonomically. The rhizoids, female plants, calyptrae and
             sporophytes are described for the first time. These features are closest to those of Metzgeria (Metzgeriaceae)
             and support the placement of Vandiemenia within the Metzgeriaceae.

             KEYWORDS: Hepatic, Vandiemeniaceae, Vandiemenia ratkowskiana, Tasmania, taxonomy.

                                                                                           DISCUSSION

                                                                  The original description of Vandiemenia was restricted to
                                                                  the anatomy and morphology of male plants and this
                                                                  limited any conclusive inference to its taxonomic rela-
                                                                  tionship. The following discussion comprises a detailed
                                                                  appraisal of both male and female characters of
                                                                  Vandiemenia, which critically compares its affinity to
                                                                  related taxa and thereby clarifies its taxonomic status.

                                                              SUMMARY

             The endemic Tasmanian hepatic Vandiemenia ratkowskiana Hewson, originally referred to the family
             Vandiemeniaceae (Marchantiophyta), is restudied taxonomically. The rhizoids, female plants, calyptrae and
             sporophytes are described for the first time. These features are closest to those of Metzgeria (Metzgeriaceae)
             and support the placement of Vandiemenia within the Metzgeriaceae.

             KEYWORDS: Hepatic, Vandiemeniaceae, Vandiemenia ratkowskiana, Tasmania, taxonomy.

                                                                                           DISCUSSION

                                                                  The original description of Vandiemenia was restricted to
                                                                  the anatomy and morphology of male plants and this
                                                                  limited any conclusive inference to its taxonomic rela-
                                                                  tionship. The following discussion comprises a detailed
                                                                  appraisal of both male and female characters of
                                                                  Vandiemenia, which critically compares its affinity to
                                                                  related taxa and thereby clarifies its taxonomic status.
