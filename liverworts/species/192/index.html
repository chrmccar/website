BAZZANIA_AUSTRALIA_2019 (1 entry)

Plants yellow–green to dark green, forming dense, overlapping mats on tree trunks and rocks. Shoots to ~3.0 mm wide, terminal branching common, pseudodichotomous. Leaves tightly curled to the ventral side of the stem when dry but spreading widely when moist, rather brittle, to ~1.8 mm long and 0.6 mm wide, 3-fid, the lobes spreading widely, margins entire; cells in mid-leaf shortly rectangular, mostly 30–40 × 25 μm, with small to medium trigones forming a subvitta of larger cells. Underleaves mostly longer than wide, 0.7–1.0 × 0.65–0.8 mm, patent to erect; very narrowly connate with the leaves on both sides, or one side only; cells in the lower 1/3 to 7/8 chlorophyllose. Distribution: Australia (Tasmania), New Zealand (Stewart Island).
