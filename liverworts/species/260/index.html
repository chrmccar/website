more than 0.5.mm wide. The leaves are dark brown and densely
overlapping. The lobules are tall and helmet-shaped, standing at a
wide angle to the stem so that the stylus that connects each lobule to its
leaf can be seen clearly. The oval underleaves are deeply bifid.
