Branching common, the branches long, mostly of Frullania type; ventral-intercalary type occasional. Stems with surface striate-papillose, in cross section with a 1 -layered hyalodermis of small cells with the exposed wall very thin and (1)2 subhyaloderm cell layers with strongly thickened, sometimes slightly reddish brown walls; medullary cells thin-walled, on average slightly larger. Rhizoids usually present, in bundles from the stem at the ventral base of the leaves. Leaves markedly stiff and brittle, easily tearing, loosely to densely imbricate, bilobed to 0.6-0.75, sharply keeled, the keel ± straight, oriented oblique to stem or nearly at right angles.
