Although Zoopsis includes some of the smallest liverworts in the world, they are
among the easiest to recognise. The shoots of Z. argentea form flat green mats in wet
forest, rainforest and fern gullies. The leaves are reduced to mere stubs, each of two
large cells, giving them a slightly lobed appearance; they are arranged alternately
along the stem and are inserted longitudinally. Although it is not obvious under a
hand lens, there is a .long, hair-like papilla curled over the surface of each leaf cell.
Although underleaves are present they are invisible under a hand lens, as they consist
of a pair of miniscule, sausage-shaped cells.
