The characteristic marsupium of this species is 3-4 mm long and hairy, and arises from a short branch at the base of the stem. However, the marsupium tends to break away if the plant is pulled from the substratum, making identification harder. The dark green shoots are 10-30 mm tall, usually erect and often mixed with other bryophytes on logs and tree trunks in Nothofagus forest. The stems are almost bare of leaves at the 'base, becoming leafier towards the apex but with the largest leaves often in mid­ stem.
