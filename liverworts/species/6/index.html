Discussion: Engel (1968) reported Balantiopsis diplophylla for Lord Howe Island, citing Watts 91c, originally described as B. pusilla Steph. Engel considered the only extant type material of B. pusilla to be a poorly developed specimen of B. diplophylla (Hook.f. & Taylor) Mitt. at the extreme end of a series of forms he described, although he did not illustrate this form. Since then several additional collections of Balantiopsis have been made on Lord Howe Island, and all match B. pusilla as described by Stephani. Since no other forms have been found on Lord Howe Island, as one would expect if B. pusilla were only a form of B. diplophylla, there may be no grounds for maintaining the synonymy. However, in the absence of fertile specimens and genetic data, I am reluctant to disregard Engel’s treatment, and therefore accept for the present that the Lord Howe Island plants belong in B. diplophylla.
