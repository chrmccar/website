discussion: COMMENTS The only report of this species in New Caledonia is from E. G. Paris (1906, 1910) as Chiloscyphus cymbaliferus. The two specimens in his herbarium kept at Rennes University (REN) are not different from Tetracymbaliella comptonii. Therefore, T. cymbalifera must be removed from the New Caledonian flora.
