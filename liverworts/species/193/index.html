BAZZANIA_AUSTRALIA_2019 (1 entry)

Plants mid- to dark green or brownish-green, forming loose mats on tree trunks. Shoots up to 7 mm wide, stems ~180–200 μm in diameter. Leaves triangular-ovate, mostly 2.5–3.5 mm long and 1.2–1.5 mm wide, apex distinctly 3-fid, the lobes shortly triangular, rather blunt. Underleaves large, quadrate to rectangular, narrowing slightly towards the apex, typically 0.8–1.0 mm wide × 1.2–1.4 mm long, margins entire to weakly crenate; narrowly connate with a leaf on one side only; 1 or 2 marginal rows of hyaline cells in the apical half. Distribution: Australia (Tasmania), New Zealand. [Note: DB alternate spelling of novae-zelandiae; same taxon]
