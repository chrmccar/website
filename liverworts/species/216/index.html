Chiloscyphus Corda Engel (2010) revised Chiloscyphus for Australasia (temperate Australia and New Zealand), recognising 38 species from the region. However, he did not include Lord Howe Island in this revision and thus did not consider Chiloscyphus howeanus (Steph.) J.J.Engel and R.M.Schust., which is endemic to the island. This species has not been reviewed since it was first published, and has never been illustrated. It is therefore fully described and illustrated here. Chiloscyphus howeanus was described (as Lophocolea howeana) by Stephani (1914) from a collection made by William Watts in 1911 (Stephani and Watts 1914). Engel and Schuster (1985) transferred the species to Chiloscyphus with numerous other species as part of a re- evaluation of the family Geocalycaceae, in which Chiloscyphus was then placed. The species clearly belongs in Chiloscyphus on account of the characters described below. The only other Chiloscyphus species known to occur on Lord Howe Island is C. semiteres, which is by far the more common of the two species on the island.
