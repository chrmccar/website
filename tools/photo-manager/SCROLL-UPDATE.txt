PHOTO MANAGER SCROLL FIX — FOR YOUR EXISTING WORKING FOLDER

1. Close the Photo Manager browser tab and its terminal window.
2. Extract Photo-Manager-Scroll-Fix.zip into a temporary folder (Downloads is fine).
3. Copy editor.css and editor.html from that extracted folder.
4. Paste both files into your EXISTING Tasmania-Photo-Manager working folder,
   where START-WINDOWS.bat, photo_manager.py and LOCAL_COLLECTION.json are.
5. Choose Replace the files in the destination.
6. Open START-WINDOWS.bat again (or START-MAC.command on Mac).

The photo list on the left now scrolls inside its own panel. Hover over the list
and use your mouse wheel. The map stays in place. The form has its own scroll too.

This update contains only the interface files. It does NOT contain a catalogue,
photographs, originals, or drafts. Do not replace your working folder with a fresh
starter download; keep your existing folder and replace ONLY these two files.
