ABOUT THE PROJECT

Tasmania Through Time

A small, independently curated photographic atlas exploring Tasmania's past, one place at a time.

Markers indicate an original camera position where known, or the location depicted by the photograph. Arrows indicate estimated viewing directions, not exact photograph footprints.
